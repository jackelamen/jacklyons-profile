02 — The Story
2005

The Newsroom

Morgan State gave me a Communication Studies degree in 2004. CNN in Atlanta gave me everything else. I started as a video journalist — the voice on the studio floor directing anchors and crew through live broadcasts — and in 2006 became Satellite Feeds Coordinator, watching every incoming feed from CNN affiliates around the world. Then documentaries: associate producer, production assistant, photojournalist, pieces for CNN.com. Five years of working with international bureaus left me wanting a career on that scale.

2010

Seoul

So in 2010 I moved to Seoul, South Korea, as an elementary English teacher — planning lessons and tracking the progress of students from first through sixth grade. I meant to stay a while. I never really left. The city's music scene pulled me in almost immediately; I taught myself to DJ and started working bars and clubs across town.

2012

Sori Zilla

DJing led to throwing my own parties in Itaewon and Hongdae, and the stage time cracked open a hidden passion — rapping. In 2012 I picked a name for all of it: Sori Zilla, from 소리질러 ("sori-jilleo"), what a Korean crowd shouts when it wants you to make noise. I never produced an album, but I've been featured on songs and have performed at concerts and festivals across Korea.

2014

On Screen

In 2014 I started acting — professionally, and mostly by accident. Since then I've appeared in films, dramas, and commercials, sharing scenes with some of Korea's biggest names: Ha Jung-woo, Lee Jung-jae, Ra Mi-ran, Nam Goong-min, Lee Sung-kyung, and director Bong Joon-ho. I act less often now, but I still show up when the right role finds me.

2016

Building FAME

I'd already spent 2015 as COO of the nightlife startup Entourage Seoul when I co-founded FAME Entertainment Group — Fashion, Art, Music, Entertainment. We produced concerts, fashion shows, variety shows, and live competitions, and consulted on nightlife venues. The mission: kill the "starving artist" myth, and prove creativity is as valuable as medicine or law. In 2020 the pandemic shut the whole events industry down, FAME included. People still ask when it's coming back. I still hope so.

2020

The Pivot

With the stages dark, I taught myself a new trade: corporate marketing and global commerce. I became a consultant, communications director, and corporate matchmaker — connecting Korean startups and multinationals with partners in the U.S., Europe, and Southeast Asia — and launched The 2X Project, a sub-brand of my family's business. The skills carried over. A good event and a good market entry both come down to a room full of people who want to be there.

2021

Going Global

From there the map only got wider. I led global strategy for BEE, helped government-sponsored Korean SMEs sharpen their international marketing at Innsbruck, and — as CEO of Gene Story's Singapore company, its logistics HQ — pitched investors and distributors around the world while building offices from scratch. Then ScreenCoach, wearing every hat a startup has. Today I'm Global Sales Director at Inspien and Global Business Development Manager at Innsbruck. Still making noise — just in more time zones.
