Sori-jilleo — "make some noise." That's the phrase I built a name around back in 2012, and it's still the thread running through everything since: nightlife, film, photography, business, and now, this record of it all.

01 — The Story
2010

Seoul

I grew up in the U.S., but in 2010 I moved to Seoul, South Korea — meant to stay a while, never really left. Everything else in this story happens here.

2012

Sori Zilla

I taught myself to DJ, worked Seoul's bars and clubs, then started throwing my own parties in Itaewon and Hongdae. That stage time cracked open a hidden passion — rapping. In 2012 I picked the name Sori Zilla, from 소리질러 ("sori-jilleo") — what a Korean crowd shouts when they want you to make noise. It's been my brand and my mission ever since.

2014

On Screen

In 2014 I started acting — professionally, and mostly by accident. Since then I've shared scenes with some of Korea's biggest names: Ha Jung-woo, Lee Jung-jae, Ra Mi-ran, Nam Goong-min, Lee Sung-kyung, and director Bong Joon-ho. I act less often these days, but I still show up when the right role finds me.

2015

Building FAME

In 2015 I became COO of Entourage Seoul, a nightlife startup producing concerts and events across the city. Two years later I founded FAME Entertainment Group — Fashion, Art, Music, Entertainment — to help creative entrepreneurs treat their work like a real business. FAME ran until the pandemic forced it to close in 2020. I still hope to bring it back.

2020

The Pivot

When entertainment shut down, I pivoted — into corporate marketing, communications, and global trade, connecting Korean companies with partners across the U.S., Europe, and Southeast Asia. That work became The 2X Project, my family's newest venture. Same instinct as always: find the noise worth making, then go make it.
